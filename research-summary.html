Waste to Wealth

Course research project | Spinach micropropagation

Research focus: evaluating locally available organic waste as lower-cost inputs for spinach tissue-culture media.

Working hypothesis

Processed eggshell, banana peel, and potato peel can contribute useful nutrients to a spinach micropropagation medium while reducing dependence on higher-cost inputs.

Materials and comparison design

  • Organic inputs: eggshell, banana peel, and potato peel.
  • Comparison: conventional control medium versus waste-derived treatment media.
  • Outcome measures: contamination, culture survival, shoot response, rooting response, and cost per culture vessel.

Culture-media parameters to document

Final protocol records should include sterilisation method, treatment concentration, pH, temperature, light conditions, culture duration, subculture interval, and number of replicates. These values must be completed from the laboratory protocol and results log.

Cost-reduction analysis

Calculate cost per culture vessel for each treatment and compare it with the control using:

Cost reduction (%) = (control cost - treatment cost) / control cost x 100

Results and percentage reductions should be added after experimental validation. This summary intentionally avoids unverified figures.
